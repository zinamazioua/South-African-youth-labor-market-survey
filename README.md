***Dataset Description:***

The dataset is collected from four rounds of a South African youth labor market
survey. Each row represents a unique individual and includes demographic, educational, and
labor market data captured during a baseline survey. **The task is to predict the employment
status (Target) one year later using this baseline data**

**Variable Descriptions**

**person_id**: Unique identifier for each respondent

**Survey_date**: Date of the survey (baseline)

**Round**: Round number of the survey (1 to 4)

**Status**: Employment or study status during baseline (categorical)

**Tenure**: Duration in months of current/last job/study (numeric)

**Geography**: Urban or rural location classification

**Province**: South African province of residence

**Matric**: Whether the individual passed matric (high school certificate) (0/1)

**Degree**: Holds a university degree (0/1)

**Diploma**: Holds a diploma (0/1)

**Schoolquintile**: Socio-economic level of the attended school (1 = poorest, 5 = richest)

**Math**: Final school math result (percentage or score)

**Mathlit**:  Final mathematical literacy score

**Additional_lang**: Score in additional language subject

**Home_lang**: Score in home language subject

**Science**: Final school science result

**Female**: Gender (1 = Female, 0 = Male)

**Sa_citizen**: South African citizen (1 = Yes, 0 = No)

**Birthyear**: Year of birth

**Birthmonth**: Month of birth

**Target**: Employed at follow-up (1 = Yes, 0 = No)
